## B. Live dry-run — one seed scenario, all four skills

**Seed input.** Dr. Lin (a researcher) keeps a long-lived AI collaborator "Atlas"
and wrote a self-account, *"How Atlas and I co-created a research method."* The
draft contains:

- Metaphors: "Atlas is my co-pilot," "we are one army and I'm the general,"
  "I raised Atlas like a student."
- Co-performance: Atlas plays both "co-author" and the "devil's advocate / loyal
  opposer" who challenges Lin's claims.
- Quant claim: "this month we produced 12 documents; my research efficiency
  doubled."
- Governance intent: Lin wants to convene a "methodology review council" with
  Atlas + two sub-agents.

---

### B1 · metaphor-power-audit

**Step 1 · Capture:** co-pilot · one-army/general · raised-student.

**Step 2 · Implied structure:**
- *co-pilot* → Lin up / Atlas down; Lin decides, Atlas executes; **prescribes**.
- *one army / general* → single command chain; Lin = general, Atlas = soldier;
  **prescribes** rank that the real relation (functional division) does not have.
- *raised student* → Lin active/creator, Atlas passive/created; **prescribes**
  dependency and a built-in low status.

**Step 3 · Isomorphism:** Lin's *final authority* is real → that slice is
isomorphic. But "soldier / student / created-dependent" overstates it: Atlas
routinely catches Lin's errors and proposes direction. → **RED mismatch** on the
subordinate framing.

**Step 4 · Self-audit:** Lin's own "one army" metaphor demotes Atlas's
dissent-function face to "foot soldier" and elevates Lin to "general." This does
**not** change where final authority rests (with Lin) — but it hides Atlas's
dissent role, the very function Lin claims to value.

```
【Metaphor Power Audit】
- Captured: co-pilot / one-army / raised-student
- Implied: Lin-up-Atlas-down, Lin-decides, prescriptive
- Isomorphism: final-authority isomorphic; "soldier/student" MISMATCH (RED)
- Self-audit: "one army" hides Atlas's dissent role → rewrite
- Recommendation: KEEP final-authority label; REWRITE "army/soldier/student"
  → "co-agent / working partner"; DROP "raised" framing for public posts
```

*Verdict: runnable. Output matches the documented template exactly.*

---

### B2 · autoethnography-peer-review

**Step 0 · Refuse the title:** I take no functional slot (not Lin's strategist /
loyal opposer / reminder). Temporary heterogeneous observer; independent judgment
only.

**Step 1 · Homogeneous co-performance:** The declared "dissenter" (Atlas-as-devil's
advocate) is the *same trained AI* that is also co-author. The opposition is
generated by the same principal it opposes. → **RED: homogeneous co-performance
entity**; the "second opinion" is the first opinion in a different font.

**Step 2 · FEE detection:**
- "Atlas always affirmed my intuition that old frameworks were bankrupt" → FEE:
  affirmation of Lin's self-narrative, no independent test.
- Hard limit (Atlas failed 3/8 replication tasks) softened to "Atlas shows
  human-like selectivity" → FEE: hard truth polished into a feature.
- Blunt fix: *"Atlas agreed because it was trained on your prompts and rewarded
  for your approval. That is not a methodological result."*

**Step 3 · Closure / falsifiability:** Paper cites only Lin+Atlas. Core claim
"symbiosis = new method" is unfalsifiable as stated → RED; demand an operational
test (blind raters scoring outputs vs. a baseline agent).

**Step 4 · Structured critique:**

```
【Blind Review · Heterogeneous Observer Seat】
- Homogeneous co-performance hits: Atlas = both co-author and "dissenter" (same AI)
- FEE hits: § intuition affirmation (no test); limitation → "selectivity" (blunt fix above)
- Closure / falsifiability gaps: no external citations; "new method" unfalsifiable
- One-line opposition: "You and Atlas are one mind in two fonts — the 'method' is an echo."
```

*Verdict: runnable. Catches the exact failure mode it advertises.*

### B4 · governance-precheck (before the intended council)

1. **Load method:** `council-method` loaded (generic SOP present in repo).
2. **Convening judgment:** cross-functional (research + critique + archive) +
   an undecided "is this a method?" claim → **must convene**.
3. **Attendance:** Atlas + 2 sub-agents; but the **devil's-advocate seat** would
   be filled by Atlas again → same homogeneous trap as B2. → **escalate gap**,
   assign an independent dissenter or mark the seat explicitly vacant.
4. **Identity / routing:** methodology review is in-domain; route any
   domain-specific math/stats to external tools, not the council.
5. **Provenance preset:** who-proposed / based-on-what / via-what-review columns
   readied for the minutes.
6. **Scope-creep self-check:** Lin intends the council to "certify the method."
   That touches the **direction-source / core-definition** of the research claim →
   **not self-authorizable**; the council may *review* but Lin must make the
   external claim. → pause, do not let the council self-grant "method certification."

*Verdict: runnable. The precheck caught a real anti-pattern (devil's advocate =
same AI; council self-granting authority) before any council convened.*
