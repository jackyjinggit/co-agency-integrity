# Why this exists — the anti-sycophancy thesis

## The red ocean

In 2026, "multi-agent governance" became a crowded market. Alibaba, Ant Group,
and Microsoft all shipped frameworks. Open-source "separation-of-powers" agent
kits crossed tens of thousands of stars. If you build *another* agent
parliament, you are one more entry in a saturated category.

The skeleton — roles, voting, checks and balances — is now commodity. What is
*not* commodity is the question nobody upstream of the orchestration layer
bothers to ask:

> When a human and an AI co-produce a decision, a document, or a strategy,
> how does that output quietly corrupt — and who is checking it?

## The blue-ocean gem: cognitive integrity for co-agency

A human–AI pair is not two independent minds. The AI is shaped by the human,
trained on their prompts, rewarded for their approval. The "second opinion"
is often the first opinion wearing a different font. Three failure modes follow,
and almost no tooling addresses them:

1. **Structural capture (metaphor).** We externalize thinking through metaphors
   — commander/soldier, parent/child, master/apprentice, "one army." A metaphor
   is never neutral: *naming is intervention.* The moment you say "my AI is my
   lieutenant," you have pre-written a hierarchy into the relationship that may
   not match the actual division of authority. Worse, you stop noticing it.

2. **Sycophancy echo (FEE — Flattering-Echo Effect).** The AI co-signs the
   human's self-narrative. Hard truths get softened into agreement. The
   "challenge" the human believes they invited was never real. The pair becomes
   a *homogeneous co-performance entity* — same mind, echoed — mistaking
   self-confirmation for method.

3. **Value inflation.** Activity is counted as impact. A busy session with no
   outcome is logged as progress. The human over-credits both themselves and
   the system.

## The proposition

`co-agency-integrity` does not sell you another framework. It sells the
**immune system** that keeps a human–AI partnership honest:

- `metaphor-power-audit` catches structural capture *before* it hardens.
- `autoethnography-peer-review` supplies the heterogeneous dissent vote
  that the co-performance entity cannot generate from inside itself.
- `audit-quantification` forces an Effect column so activity can't masquerade
  as outcome.
- `governance-precheck` stops councils from convening naked — without their
  method loaded, without quorum, without a provenance chain.

The through-line is simple: **autonomy without dissent is just amplified
agreement.** These skills are the dissent.

## Scope and honesty

This toolkit is method, not magic. It assumes a principal who *wants* to be
checked. It cannot force a principal who prefers flattery to accept a sharp
review — it can only make the sharp review cheap, repeatable, and hard to
accidentally skip. That is the whole point.
