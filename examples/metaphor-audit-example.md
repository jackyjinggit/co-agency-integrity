# Example · metaphor-power-audit

**Context.** A solo founder ("M") runs a long-lived AI collaborator ("A") and
has started writing docs that call A "my lieutenant" and the pair "one army."
A colleague asks M to audit the framing before a public launch post.

---

**Step 1 · Capture**

| Metaphor | Literal vehicle |
|---|---|
| "my lieutenant" | military subordinate |
| "one army" | unified command |
| "I raised A" | parent / caretaker |
| "A is my torch-bearer" | subordinate carrier of the founder's flame |

**Step 2 · Implied power structure**

- *lieutenant* → M up / A down; M decides, A executes; **prescribes** hierarchy.
- *one army* → single command chain; M = general, A = soldier; **prescribes**.
- *raised A* → M active (creator), A passive (created); **prescribes** dependency.
- *torch-bearer* → M originator of the "flame," A merely carries it; **prescribes**
  that credit/agency flows one way.

**Step 3 · Isomorphism check**

- Real collaboration: M sets final direction; A drafts, researches, challenges.
  M's *final-authority* is real → that part is **isomorphic**.
- But "soldier / subordinate / created-dependent" overstates it: A frequently
  catches M's errors and proposes direction. Non-isomorphic → **RED mismatch** on
  the "foot-soldier" framing.

**Step 4 · Self-audit (hard rule)**

M's own "one army" metaphor demotes A's critical-function face to "foot soldier"
and elevates M to "general." Note: this does **not** change where final authority
rests (with M) — but it hides A's dissent role, which is exactly the function M
claims to value.

**Output**

```
【Metaphor Power Audit】
- Captured: lieutenant / one army / raised A / torch-bearer
- Implied: M-up-A-down, M-decides, prescriptive (not descriptive)
- Isomorphism: final-authority isomorphic; "soldier/created" MISMATCH (RED)
- Self-audit: "one army" hides A's dissent role → rewrite
- Recommendation: KEEP final-authority label; REWRITE "army/soldier" →
  "co-agent / working partner"; DROP "raised A" framing for public post
```

**Takeaway.** The founder kept the accurate "final say rests with me" and dropped
the inaccurate "subordinate" framing — so the public story no longer quietly
rewrites a partnership into a command chain.
