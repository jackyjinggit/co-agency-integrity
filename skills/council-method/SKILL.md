# council-method (generic SOP)

A minimal, framework-agnostic method library for convening a multi-member
governance body. It is the "method" that `governance-precheck` forces you to load
before dispatching anyone.

## §1 · Roles (generic)

- **Lead** — convenes, holds the process, does *not* pre-decide the substance.
- **Members** — one functional seat each (research, critique, execution, archive,
  etc.). Equal standing; seats are functional, not ranks.
- **Devil's advocate** — a rotating seat whose *only* job is the counterexample.
  Never omitted.

## §2 · Convening judgment

Convene a council when **any** of:

- the topic crosses a functional boundary (needs ≥2 seats),
- a red-line / irreversible-action suspicion exists,
- an undecided item is about to diffuse to others.

Single-seat tasks route directly; do not manufacture a council.

## §3 · Attendance rule

When the principal says "full," every functional seat must be present. Any
absence is escalated as a gap — never smoothed into "enough." Missing-seat
impersonation in the report is a violation.

## §4 · Deliberation rules

1. State the question before any answer.
2. Each seat speaks to its function only; no seat speaks for another.
3. The devil's advocate must land at least one live counterexample, or the
   deliberation is incomplete.
4. Dissent is logged, not resolved by silence.

## §5 · Provenance (required in every output)

Every claim carries three columns:

- **who proposed** it,
- **based on what evidence**,
- **via what review** (which seat / which check).

## §6 · Five questions (closing gate)

The final report must answer:

1. What was actually decided?
2. What was left undecided, and why?
3. Who dissented, and was it answered?
4. What evidence backs the decision?
5. What would falsify it?

## §7 · Anti-corruption note

A council serves the principal; it does not grant itself authority. Any action
that touches the direction-source, core definition, external commitment, or
a write to the canonical source must escalate to the principal — not self-authorize.
